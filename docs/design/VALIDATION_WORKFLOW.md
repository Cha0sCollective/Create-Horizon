## Build, fixture and evidence costs

Maintain targets per physical checkout. One serial CI checkout can reuse its own
normal target across revisions with Cargo tracking and deliberate invalidation;
never share a target between different physical worktrees. Source/profile/input
identities remain checked; do not repeat the known cross-worktree cache collision.

Validate frozen source, tools, executable and fixture identities at meaningful
batch boundaries and after each batch, and after any mutation. Exclusive mutation
rules and isolated state remain. Do not perform a full Git traversal around every
case when it adds no relevant assurance. Preserve failure provenance and truthful
counts; use no new general test framework.

worlds/owners. First inspect globals and per-case setup; keep incompatible fault
variants, wrong-live capture and fatal fixtures separate. Current Materialized
setup explicitly requires resource-process isolation, so batching is not silently
enabled by this policy and needs a bounded reviewed fixture change.

Preserve source/patches, build recipes, dependency/toolchain/input pins, authored
summaries, useful logs and unresolved-failure artifacts. Keep executables required
for active qualification/diagnosis; obsolete regenerable copies can expire after
verified checkpoint and inactivity. Concrete deletion still needs a bounded
